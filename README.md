# lotro
A repository for collecting various useful items for The Lord of the Rings Online.


## Plugins:

* [***InstanceLockTimer***](./plugins/InstanceLockTimer.md) - A lotro plugin that tracks your instance lock cooldowns: start a one-hour timer with a single click and keep an eye on how many you have used today.
* [***CharacterStats***](./plugins/CharacterStats.md) - A lotro plugin that shows your character's stats and equipped items in one window with collapsible groups, updates live as you change gear, and lets you share your stats to chat.
* [***ItemsTracker***](./plugins/ItemsTracker.md) - A lotro plugin that shows how many of the items you choose are in your bags: drag an item onto a slot and watch its count update live, set target counts (red-to-green progress), Ctrl+drag tracked items into the auction search box, and use the craft calculator to turn the ingredients of a profession tier of your choice (up to the current one) into live targets by picking their slots yourself.

## Installation

Download the plugin's zip from the [Releases](https://github.com/EBCEYS/lotro/releases) page and extract it into `Documents\The Lord of the Rings Online\Plugins\`, then load it in game with `/plugins load <PluginName>`. See each plugin's page for details.
